# Female model replacement

The active female atlas (`public/models/atlas-female.json`) now uses Female Atlas by Mahendra Beniwal. It contains 3,004 individually addressable meshes and 2,557 concepts across 16 systems. Geometry chunks use unique `female-atlas-*` filenames so they do not collide with previous assets. The male atlas and male geometry assets are unchanged.

Source data: HuBMAP Human Reference Atlas Female v1.5 and BodyParts3D 4.0. Both are attributed under CC BY 4.0; see `public/ATTRIBUTION.md`.

This is a data-level replacement. Visual correctness and interaction behavior still require browser testing on the target device.
